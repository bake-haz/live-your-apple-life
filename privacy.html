Privacy

The short version: this site collects almost nothing.

What the website collects

Live Your Apple Life is a static website. It does not use accounts, profiles, comment systems, or analytics cookies that identify you. Visiting a page does not require you to share any personal information.

Ask Us

The "Ask Us" page lets you email us a question. If you choose to send an email, we receive the address and message you include — the same as any normal email. We use it only to read your question and, where helpful, to write guides. We don't add you to a mailing list and we don't share your email.

Links to other sites

Some guides link to official Apple support pages for accuracy. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Changes

If this approach changes in a way that affects you, we'll say so here.
